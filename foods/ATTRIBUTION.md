## 2. Egyptian National Nutrition Institute (NNI)

- **Source**: National Nutrition Institute, General Organization for Teaching Hospitals and Institutes (GOTHI), Ministry of Health and Population, Arab Republic of Egypt. Food Composition Tables for Egypt.
- **Reference**: Food Composition Tables for Egypt (NNI, Cairo).
- **License / Usage**: Informational and research public health tables; compiled for educational, health, and fitness tracking purposes under fair use.
- **Attribution Notice**:
  Nutritional values for authentic Egyptian recipes and regional staples (e.g., Ful Medames, Koshari, Molokhia, Taameya, Baladi bread, Feteer Meshaltet) are based on the Egyptian Food Composition Tables compiled by the National Nutrition Institute (Cairo) and traditional standardized preparations.
